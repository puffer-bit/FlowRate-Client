Development build 0.6.1-dev.2610251119.

## Voice
- The voice gate fades in over 8 ms and out over 150 ms, so phrases no longer start with a click or end chopped off; a gate that changes its mind midway ramps on from where it is.
- Voices are buffered for 100 ms instead of 60 ms, so more of the jitter of a mobile or busy network is smoothed out, at the cost of 40 ms more delay.
- Every track follows the media settings of its session: Opus FEC with its expected loss, DTX and NACK of the voice.

## Video and screen share
- Lost video packets are repaired with NACK and RTX instead of waiting for the next keyframe. At 10 % loss a 20 s screen share showed about 515 of 592 frames instead of 71.
- Video streams get a 200 ms jitter buffer, so a resent packet has time to arrive; NVENC, AMF and Media Foundation encoders run in their low-latency constant-rate modes.
- A screen share can be sent and received as VP8 as well as H.264.
- After a loss, H.264 frames are held back until the next keyframe instead of drawing colored smears.
- A running screen share can be changed (another source or other settings) from the menu of the share button, without stopping it; viewers keep watching.
- A video frame no longer turns black when the interface draws a downscaled image in the same frame.
- The expanded screen stays in place when a hidden one leaves.
- Fixed a GStreamer 1.28 bug where a voice without NACK also turned NACK off for the screen share.

## Settings
- The session settings dialog has a media section: codecs of camera, screen and audio, NACK and RTX of video, NACK, FEC and DTX of audio, and the receive buffers. The settings are folded into expandable sections.
- Ctrl, Shift, Alt and Win can be used as hot keys, alone or as modifiers.
- The STUN and TURN settings are gone; upload limits and the lifetime of media tokens are edited on the storage page (protocol version 2).

## Chat
- The scroll bar can be dragged, and the middle mouse button scrolls.
- An opened image can be zoomed.
- Images free their memory once nothing shows them.
- Missed changes are applied at once, and the chat reloads after a long gap.
- Names without a colored role use the text color of the theme.
- A peer can be banned from its menu; the suspend and report items are hidden.

## Diagnostics
- Every minute the log gets the loss, jitter, late packets, NACK and PLI of every stream, how late each played voice reaches the mixer, and how the voice gate did; a burst of loss is a warning at once.
- The ICE candidate pair the media goes over is logged, with a warning when it changes.
- A network that does not take what we send (a send queue past 100 ms) is a warning.
